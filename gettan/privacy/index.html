Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 July 2026

About Support

Data stored on your device

  • Your selected city, routine goal, sun-response choice, SPF value and notification preference.
  • Completed routine history, timer recovery state and comfort check-ins.
  • The app version in which a system rating request was last attempted.

Weather and location

Location is optional. When you choose current location, iOS location and WeatherKit services are used to request local weather and UV information. You can use manual city selection instead. Get Tan does not operate an account system or a server that stores your precise location.

Notifications

If enabled, routine and SPF-label reminders are scheduled locally through iOS. You can disable notifications in system settings.

What the current build does not collect

  • No advertising identifier, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics SDK.
  • No account, contact list, microphone, camera, photo library or HealthKit access.
  • No sale of personal data.
