Get Tan

UV forecast, SPF reminders and a routine timer

Get Tan is a native iPhone and iPad lifestyle app for reviewing current UV conditions, running a user-started timer and recording local session check-ins.

Support Privacy Policy

Important boundary

Get Tan provides informational lifestyle and weather estimates. No tan is risk-free. The app does not diagnose, treat, prevent sunburn or guarantee skin protection. Follow local guidance and the directions on your sunscreen label.
