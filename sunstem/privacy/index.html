SunStem Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

SunStem is designed as an offline-first native iPhone and iPad app for off-grid power planning, monitoring evidence and local alerts.

Home Support

Data stored on your device

  • Saved system profile, loads, manual readings and local telemetry history.
  • Local validation packets for TestFlight, field evidence, forecast inputs and alert behavior.
  • Local event names and timestamps used to understand whether key app flows were reached.
  • Victron AES keys and optional VRM tokens stored in the iOS Keychain.

Data sent outside your device

  • If you run Open-Meteo forecast import, SunStem sends region-derived latitude and longitude to Open-Meteo to request hourly cloud-cover forecast data.
  • If you choose to share an export through the iOS share sheet, the destination receives the content you selected.

Data SunStem does not collect in the current build

  • No account is required before first value.
  • No advertising tracking and no sale of personal data.
  • No contact list, photo library, microphone, camera, HealthKit or precise device GPS access.
  • No exported Victron AES keys or VRM tokens in validation evidence packets.

App boundary

SunStem provides planning estimates and read-only evidence exports. It does not certify electrical installations and does not control chargers, inverters or BMS hardware.
