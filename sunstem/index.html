SunStem

Off-grid power cockpit for iPhone and iPad

SunStem helps RV, van, caravan, boat and cabin owners review battery risk, local energy assumptions and read-only field evidence.

Support Privacy Policy

Native app scope

SunStem is an offline-first native iOS app. It provides planning estimates and read-only evidence exports; it does not certify electrical installations or control chargers, inverters or BMS hardware.

SunStem is a native iPhone and iPad app. These pages are static App Store support and privacy pages, not a web app surface.
